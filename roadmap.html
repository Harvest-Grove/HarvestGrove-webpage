Roadmap

From a small patch of land to the mysteries beyond the archipelago.

  • Game Base

    Start with nothing and build a new life on your home island. Grow crops, gather resources, raise animals, craft tools, and shape your own little farm.

    Explore the nearby Forest Island and get to know your first companion: a loyal cat by your side.

  • Cave Island

    There's more to the archipelago than forests and fields. Set sail for Cave Island and dig deeper into the resources hidden beneath the surface.

    New buildings, crops, trees, and a new companion await - this time, a loyal dog.

  • Seasons

    The world begins to change with the seasons. Spring, summer, autumn, and winter each bring new conditions for farming, nature, and your animals.

    Take to the road with new ridable animals, including horses and ostriches.

  • City Island

    The archipelago isn't empty after all. Reach City Island, meet its poeple, trade goods, take on quests and help breathe new life into the city.

  • Desert Island

    Leave the familiar behind and venture into a harsher environment. Discover new crops, trees, animals, and buildings while meeting the people who have made the desert their home.

  • Lore Expansion

    The islands hold secrets that go far beyond farming. Uncover traces of a forgotten civilization, investigate ancient devices, and piece together what happened to the people who came before.

    Four new islands will open the door to the deeper mysteries of the archipelago.

  • Steam Launch

    Harvest Grove arrives on Steam. Continue building your farm, exploring the archipelago, and uncovering the secrets hidden among its islands.
